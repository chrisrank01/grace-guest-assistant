61 taps in six weeks, 4th most tapped of the thirteen — and it has never been offered first. Every one of those taps came from a guest tapping through to find it. Guests want this answer badly enough to go looking for it. It is not in the widget’s opening list, so everyone who read it had to tap another question first and find it underneath.

Plan your visit already offers five questions, which is the most it can show. Nothing can be added without something coming out. The candidate is “Can I watch online instead?” — offered to every guest for six weeks and tapped three times. That one is not landing: every guest is shown it and almost nobody opens it. Whether that is the wording, or simply a question people do not have, is a judgement only you can make — but either way it is the weakest thing occupying a slot.

How to make the swap
  1. Open the corpus spreadsheet, PLACEMENT tab.
  2. Find the Plan Your Visit row, column Starter question IDs (3–5).
  3. Swap watch-online for pets-firearms in that cell.
  4. Save and leave the sheet alone for half an hour. It publishes itself on the next run.

The order you type them in is the order guests see. Nothing else needs changing, and no one has to deploy anything.
