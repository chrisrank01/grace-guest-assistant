Plan Your Visit

Come as you are. Sit wherever you like.

Visiting a church for the first time can feel like walking into a room where everyone already knows the rules. Here's everything you'd want to know before Sunday — and the orange button in the corner will answer the rest.

Sunday mornings

Two identical services, 9:00 and 11:00 a.m. Doors and coffee open half an hour early. Plan on about 75 minutes.

Arriving

Guest parking is marked in orange by the main doors.

Kids

Nursery through 5th grade during both services.

Wearing

Jeans, a dress, whatever you had on already.

Still have a question?

Tap the orange button in the bottom-right corner. It's tap-only — there's no box to type into, and every answer was written by our team.
